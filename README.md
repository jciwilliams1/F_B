## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases a collection of SQL business analyses developed as part of the **Business Reporting Automation Career Accelerator (BRA CAP)**. The analyses focused on supporting sales and operational reporting for the Food & Beverage department of a performing arts nonprofit.

Using organizational data stored in Snowflake, I developed SQL queries to examine product profitability, event transaction activity, and quarterly sales performance.

The goal was to transform business data into meaningful reporting outputs that support operational planning, revenue analysis, and executive decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The reporting requests focused on three questions:

- Which **food and beverage products generated the strongest sales and profit margins?**
- Which **events and entertainment genres had the highest transaction activity?**
- How could leadership **review box office and food and beverage revenue together by fiscal quarter?**

## 💻 Project Work & Deliverables

The following three analyses address business reporting requests involving product profitability, event activity, and quarterly revenue performance.

---

### 📊 1. Sales Analysis: By Item Sales

**Business Scenario**

Business leaders wanted to identify which food and beverage products generated the highest sales and profit margins across entertainment events.

**Analysis Overview**

This query provides an item-level sales summary for every product sold during an event, along with the corresponding profit margin for a specified time period.

**Business Value**

- Evaluate product profitability
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products
- Optimize pricing strategies
- Improve inventory planning
- Maximize food and beverage revenue

**SQL Query**

[View By Item Sales Query](SQL_Queries/top_event_by_genre.sql)

---

### 🎟️ 2. Event Performance: By Transaction

**Business Scenario**

Leadership wanted to compare customer purchasing activity across events to determine which entertainment performances generated the greatest transaction volume.

**Analysis Overview**

This query returns the total number of transactions for each event, along with the corresponding entertainment genre, for a specified date range.

**Business Value**

- Measure customer engagement
- Compare event popularity
- Analyze genre performance
- Identify high-performing events
- Support scheduling and marketing decisions

**SQL Query**

[View By Transaction Query](SQL_Queries/top_genre_by_category.sql)

---

### 💰 3. Executive Dashboard: Sales & Collections

**Business Scenario**

Executive leadership required a quarterly sales report aligned to the organization's July–June fiscal year.

**Analysis Overview**

This query generates a quarterly sales report by combining box office revenue with food and beverage sales into a single executive reporting solution.

**Business Value**

- Monitor quarterly revenue performance
- Analyze fiscal-year trends
- Compare revenue streams
- Support executive reporting
- Improve financial forecasting

**SQL Query**

[View Sales & Collections Query](SQL_Queries/top_item_by_category.sql)

## 📈 Business Value & Reporting Applications

The SQL analyses developed throughout this project support business reporting and operational decision-making by helping the organization:

- **Evaluate Product Performance:** Identify high-performing and underperforming food and beverage products based on sales and profitability.
- **Analyze Event Activity:** Compare transaction volumes across entertainment events and genres to better understand customer purchasing behavior.
- **Monitor Revenue Trends:** Examine quarterly box office and food and beverage revenue within the organization's July–June fiscal year.
- **Support Operational Planning:** Provide information that can guide inventory management, product pricing, and event planning.
- **Strengthen Executive Reporting:** Organize sales and operational data into meaningful reporting outputs that support leadership decision-making.
